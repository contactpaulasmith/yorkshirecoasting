Bus to The Courtyard (from Scarborough Train Station)
You can take a direct bus from Scarborough Station to Peasholm Park in about 5–9 minutes, with services departing every 30 minutes.
Bus Route Details
•	Departure Stop: West Square Stop V, near Scarborough train station 
•	Arrival Stop: Peasholm Park bus stop, just a 1-minute walk to the park entrance 
•	Bus Operators: Yorkshire Coastliner operates the direct service. Lines 13 and 843 serve Peasholm Park.   Every 30 minutes throughout the day.  Approximately 5 minutes journey,  Cost: Around £2–£3 per trip 
•	Walking: The distance is roughly 1 mile, which takes about 20 minutes on foot

Transport to other towns
Buses from the Scarborough train station leave approximately hourly the 840 bus takes you to York, Malton, Pickering.  Or you might consider the train for a faster journey. You can catch regular trains to local places like Filey, Bridlington, Malton and York from Scarborough.
